## Covariates

This folder contains datasets on the covariates used in the analyses.
- GDPC1.xlsx: Real Gross Domestic Product, Billions of Chained 2017 Dollars, Annual, Seasonally Adjusted Annual Rate from Federal Reserve Economic Data, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis
- MER_T10_01.csv: Monthly Energy Review, Renewables, from U.S. Energy Information Administration
